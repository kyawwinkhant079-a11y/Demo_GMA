About Golden Myanmar Alliance

Connecting & Growing Together With Myanmar

Golden Myanmar Alliance (GMA) registered as an independent entity in March 2016 under the Ministry of National Planning and Economic Development of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar.

Focusing on medical products and hospital utilities trading, GMA achieved major milestones across government hospital tenders within its first operating year.

In 2017, to expand countrywide commercial distribution and broaden its industrial expertise, GMA merged with Myanmar Paradigm Group (MPG). Today, MPG comprises four main divisions: Trading, Distribution, Government Projects, and Services.
